# Phase 1j-c1: News sources and event confirmation Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fetch company news from free sources and use it to show, next to every adjusted, flagged or noted corporate event, what the exchange and the press said about it, so a person can see why a price moved. The news specialist (sentiment) is Phase 1j-c2 and reads the same sources.

**Architecture:** `athena.news` holds a `NewsItem` model, three source callables (NSE corporate announcements, publisher RSS feeds, Google News RSS) behind one `NewsHub` that tolerates failures and removes duplicates, and a pure `confirm_events` that matches events to headlines near their dates. `CheckedHistory` exposes the events it found; `live_service` joins events, news and confirmation into dashboard notes. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-phase-1j-data-integrity-design.md` (layer 4). Source check Oct 10, 2026.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.13, requests and the standard-library XML parser (no new dependencies), pytest.

## Global Constraints

- **News never supplies a ratio or changes a price.** It only confirms that something was reported, or shows what was reported near an unexplained break. Nothing in this phase touches prices.
- **Google News RSS is off by default** (its robots.txt disallows crawling). It runs only when the setting `ATHENA_GOOGLE_NEWS` is `1` (read with `athena.llm.envfile.read_setting`, so a shell variable or the `.env` the owner edits both work); the dashboard says when it is off. At most about 1 request a second; at most 5 event lookups per view.
- NSE announcements reuse the NSE client conventions (browser User-Agent, Referer `https://www.nseindia.com/`, session primed with a GET of the home page, status ignored). BSE, Reddit and GDELT are not used (blocked or unreliable in the source check). No stealth or evasion tooling, no X/Twitter.
- Publisher feeds used (verified live Oct 10): ET stocks `https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/rssfeeds/2146842.cms`, ET markets `https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/rssfeeds/1977021501.cms`, Business Standard `https://www.business-standard.com/rss/markets-106.rss` and `.../companies-101.rss`, Mint `https://www.livemint.com/rss/markets` and `/rss/companies`, BusinessLine `https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/feeder/default.rss` and `/companies/feeder/default.rss`. They hold only the last 1-3 days, so they serve current headlines, never past events. Moneycontrol (stale since 2024) and NDTV Profit (404) are excluded.
- Text from any source is untrusted data: stored and shown as text, never executed, never treated as instructions (TRD prompt-injection rule). Titles are cut to 300 characters.
- Any source failing is a note, never an error; the rest still run. Existing tests keep passing (1030 passed, 73 skipped at the start).

## Tasks

1. **Model and name matching.** `NewsItem(title, publisher, published: datetime, link, source, snippet="")`. `company_names("Vedanta Limited") -> ("Vedanta", "Vedanta Limited")` (drop Ltd, Limited, Corporation, Corp, Industries only when a base name remains), and `mentions(item, names)` with case-insensitive whole-word matching (so "Idea" does not match "ideal"). Tests: names for Vedanta Limited, Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited, State Bank of India; whole-word matching; the title is cut at 300 characters.
2. **NSE announcements** (`NseAnnouncements(session=None, today=...)`, callable `(symbol, since, until) -> list[NewsItem]`; fake-session tests as for `NseActions`). Endpoint `https://www.nseindia.com/api/corporate-announcements?index=equities&symbol=X&from_date=DD-MM-YYYY&to_date=DD-MM-YYYY`; row to item: title `"{desc}: {attchmntText}"`, publisher `NSE`, time `an_dt` (`08-Oct-2026 13:11:55`, tolerate the same without seconds), link `attchmntFile`. Failure is an `AthenaError`.
3. **Publisher feeds** (`PublisherFeeds(get, clock)`, callable `(symbol, names, since, until) -> list[NewsItem]`): fetch the eight feeds, cache each for 10 minutes in-process, parse RSS 2.0 `item` (title, link, pubDate, description as snippet with tags removed), keep items whose title or snippet mentions a company name, publisher taken from the feed. One feed failing skips only that feed and is reported by the hub.
4. **Google News** (`GoogleNews(get, enabled)`, callable `(symbol, names, since, until, extra="")`): when disabled returns nothing and says why; when enabled queries `"{name}" {extra} after:{since} before:{until}` with `hl=en-IN&gl=IN&ceid=IN:en`, parses `title`, `pubDate`, `source` (publisher) and strips the trailing ` - Publisher` from the title; links stay Google's wrapper URLs. Sleeps to stay under one request a second.
5. **The hub.** `NewsHub(sources)` where each source is `(name, callable)`; `fetch(symbol, company, since, until, extra="") -> NewsResult(items, notes)`: runs every source, a failure becomes a note naming the source, drops duplicates (same normalised first 60 characters), sorts newest first. Tests: failure isolation, duplicate removal across sources, notes.
6. **Confirmation.** `confirm_events(events, items, names) -> list[Confirmation]` where each event is `(day, kind, label)` for applied events, unexplained breaks and noted rows. An item confirms when it is from 14 days before to 7 days after its date and its title or snippet mentions a keyword for the kind (`split`: split, sub-division, face value; `bonus`: bonus; `demerger`: demerger, demerged, scheme of arrangement, record date, spin; `rights`: rights; `buyback`: buyback, buy back; `unexplained`: any of the above plus merger, amalgamation, fraud, probe, results, crash, halt, circuit). Status `confirmed` (at least one item, with up to 2 headlines kept) or `no coverage found`. `describe_confirmations` gives one sentence per event, shown next to the price notes; for an unexplained break the sentence says the headlines are context only and nothing was adjusted. Tests use the frozen benchmark headlines.
7. **Wire it in.** `CheckedHistory.events_found(symbol)` returns the events of the last fetch (applied, unexplained, noted) as `(day, kind, label)`. `LiveSources.news` is the hub (Google News enabled from `ATHENA_GOOGLE_NEWS`). In `live_service` the price notes gain the confirmation sentences for at most the 5 most recent events, with a note when Google News is off ("press coverage beyond the last few days needs ATHENA_GOOGLE_NEWS=1"). News failing never fails a view.
8. **Record it and check live.** TRD, the changelog and the privacy/terms note (Google News off by default and why); live check on Vedanta, Kotak and Wipro with Google News on and off; full suite; commit.
